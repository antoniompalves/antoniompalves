FAQ

quick answers

Do I need to pay a monthly subscription?

No. Ready-made apps are a one-time purchase. Hosting, updates and support are available as an optional monthly plan.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Every app is designed mobile-first and works in any modern browser. You can also add it to your home screen like a native app.

Where is my data stored?

Simple apps can keep data on your device. Apps with several users or logins use a secure cloud database that only you can access.

How long does it take?

A customized ready-made app usually takes a few days. A bespoke app usually takes 2 to 6 weeks, depending on scope.

Can the app be in Portuguese?

Yes. Apps can be delivered in Portuguese, English or both.
