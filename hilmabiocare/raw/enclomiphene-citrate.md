## Product Description

Enclomiphene Citrate is a selective estrogen receptor modulator (SERM) and the trans-isomer of clomiphene citrate. It is designed to support natural testosterone production by stimulating the release of luteinizing hormone (LH) and follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH) through the hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal axis.

Unlike traditional clomiphene citrate, enclomiphene contains only the active trans-isomer, which provides a more favorable pharmacokinetic profile and reduces the risk of estrogenic side effects associated with zuclomiphene accumulation. Enclomiphene helps increase endogenous testosterone levels while maintaining fertility and supporting normal spermatogenesis.

Each tablet contains 10 mg of Enclomiphene Citrate.

Presentation: 50 uncoated tablets per bottle.

## Effects:

- Supports natural testosterone production
- Increases LH and FSH secretion
- Helps maintain fertility and spermatogenesis
- May improve energy, libido, mood, and recovery
- Supports hormonal balance in cases of secondary hypogonadism
- Less estrogenic activity compared to standard clomiphene citrate
- Does not suppress endogenous testosterone production

Recommended dosage is typically 10 mg daily. Daily intake should not exceed 25 mg unless otherwise directed by a healthcare professional.

## Side Effects:

Possible side effects may include:

- Nausea or vomiting
- Headache
- Dizziness
- Visual disturbances
- Mood changes
- Breast tenderness
- Cough or breathing difficulty
- Swelling of the face, lips, tongue, or throat
- Hormonal fluctuations
- In women: menstrual irregularities, pelvic discomfort, ovarian cysts, or vaginal bleeding

If any adverse reactions occur, discontinue use and consult a healthcare professional immediately.

## Protection:

Regular monitoring of hormone levels is recommended during use, including:

- Testosterone
- Estradiol
- LH and FSH
- Liver function markers

Enclomiphene is not recommended for individuals with liver, adrenal, or thyroid disorders. Pregnant women and individuals with ovarian cysts should avoid use.

Store in a cool, dry place between 15–25°C and protect from direct sunlight.

## Stacking:

Enclomiphene Citrate is commonly used as a standalone hormonal support compound or alongside protocols aimed at maintaining natural testosterone production and fertility support.

Potential interactions may occur with:

- Hormone therapies
- Blood thinners
- Prescription medications
- Herbal supplements and vitamins

Consult a healthcare professional before combining Enclomiphene with other compounds or medications.
