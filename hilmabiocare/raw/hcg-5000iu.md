## Product Description

HCG 5000IU represents a synthetic analogue of the naturally occurring Chorionic Gonadotropin Human peptide, pivotal in mimicking the hCG hormone found in the placenta during pregnancy.

## Effects:

This synthetic hormone is instrumental in reinvigorating testosterone levels in men, enhancing male fertility, and facilitating placental growth. Mirroring the actions of LH, FSH, and TSH, it significantly influences the secretion of gonadal steroid hormones, thereby affecting secondary sexual characteristics in males and the menstrual cycle in females, with a comparatively minor effect on FSH. Additionally, its crucial role in sustaining the corpus luteum during pregnancy highlights its comprehensive benefits.

## Side-Effects:

- Allergic Reactions: Infrequent occurrences of rashes, itching, dizziness, or facial swelling.
- Discomfort at the Injection Site: Experiences of pain, redness, or swelling may occur.
- Hormonal Overstimulation: Potential for gynecomastia or mood alterations in men.
- Water Retention: Instances of swelling attributable to water retention.
- Neurological Symptoms: Possible experiences of headaches, fatigue, or sleepiness.
- Reproductive System Effects: Prolonged usage at high doses may disrupt natural hormone production, possibly affecting fertility in the long term.

## Protection:

The dosing of HCG (Human Chorionic Gonadotropin) is contingent upon the specific application:

- For Post-Cycle Therapy (PCT): Dosages generally span from 500 IU to 2500 IU, administered two to three times a week over a few weeks, culminating in a gradual reduction.
- For Fertility Augmentation: Dosages may vary from 500 IU to 4000 IU, administered two to three times a week, with adjustments based on individual requirements and medical counsel.
- For Boosting Testosterone Levels: A weekly regimen of 500 IU to 1000 IU is typical among men, with the precise dosage being subject to specific health conditions and professional guidance.
