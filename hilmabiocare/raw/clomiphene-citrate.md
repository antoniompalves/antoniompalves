## Product Description

Originally developed for ovarian stimulation, Clomiphene Citrate (commonly known as Clomid) has quickly become a popular choice for Post Cycle Therapy (PCT) recovery. This medication belongs to the Selective Estrogen Receptor Modulator (SERM) group and is known for its ability to stimulate the production of gonadotropins, which are essential for the proper functioning of the endocrine system.

## Side Effects

The use of Clomiphene Citrate Hilma Biocare may lead to several side effects, including:

- **Nausea:** Some users may experience mild nausea.

- **Headache:** Headaches are a possible side effect during use.
- **Mood Swings:** Changes in mood and emotional fluctuations may be observed.
- **Flushing:** Some users may experience flushing or a warm sensation.
